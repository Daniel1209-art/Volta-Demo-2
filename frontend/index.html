How to play — VOLTA

Bet

Place your amount before the round begins. You can't change the bet once the round is live.

Lamp

ON — the lamp is lit, your personal multiplier grows together with the global one.

OFF — the lamp is off, your multiplier is locked and you're safe.

Crash

Lamp ON at the crash: your bet burns.

Lamp OFF at the crash: you get bet × personal multiplier.

Re-toggling

You can switch the lamp on up to 4 times per round. Each turn-on keeps stacking the multiplier on top of the previous one.

Autoplay

Set the values and the lamp toggles automatically at the right moments — handy for a pre-planned strategy.
